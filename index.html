How FeedNow Works

From surplus food to someone's meal — in just three easy steps. No paperwork, no delays.

1

List Your Food

Have surplus food? Add details like type, quantity, and when it's available for pickup.

2

Find Nearby NGOs

We match you with NGOs near you that currently need and accept your type of food.

3

Donate & Track

Send a donation request, coordinate pickup, and track your donation until it's delivered.

Platform Features

Built for Real Impact

Every feature is designed to make food donation faster, easier, and more transparent.

Location-Based Matching

Automatically find the closest NGOs based on your current location. Distance-sorted and availability-aware.

Real-Time Updates

Get instant notifications when your donation is accepted, picked up, or completed. Stay informed at every step.

Smart Matching Score

Our transparent rule-based system scores NGOs by distance, capacity, availability, and food type compatibility.

Secure & Private

Contact details are shared only after a donation is accepted. Your data is protected with row-level security.

Mobile Friendly

Works beautifully on any device — desktop, tablet, or phone. Donate food from wherever you are.

Impact Dashboard

Track your total donations, meals contributed, and communities helped — all in a beautiful dashboard.
